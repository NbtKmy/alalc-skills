# Proper-Name Word Division Exceptions

**Source:** ALA-LC Romanization Table: Japanese (2022), §6.9  
**Phase:** Phase 1 — Skill rules  
**Purpose:** Apply the special ALA-LC word-division rules for personal names, corporate names, titles of works, other proper names, titles/terms of address, and affixes attached to proper names.

> **Unofficial restatement.** This file is an unofficial, operational summary of the ALA-LC Romanization Table: Japanese (2022). It is not the official table; the source document always takes precedence. See NOTICE.md at the repository root.

> Except for the specific exceptions in §6.9, ordinary word-division rules still apply inside proper names and between proper names and other words.

## 1. General rule for personal names, corporate names, and titles of works (§6.9.1.1)

Write affixes separately from personal names, corporate names, and titles of works, including laws and musical works.

### Examples

| Japanese | Romanization |
|---|---|
| リンカーン伝 | `Rinkān den` |
| 丹羽文雄集 | `Niwa Fumio shū` |
| 国立国会図書館報 | `Kokuritsu Kokkai Toshokan hō` |
| 続日本後記 | `Shoku Nihon kōki` |
| 續群書類従 | `Zoku Gunsho ruijū` |
| 独占禁止法上 | `Dokusen kinshihō jō` |

This can override the general suffix-joining rule in §6.6.1.

---

## 2. Named ships, airplanes, trains, etc. (§6.9.1.2)

Write suffixes such as `maru` (`丸`) and `gō` (`号`) separately when they are attached to the names of ships, airplanes, trains, and similar named vehicles.

### Examples

- 浅間丸 → `Asama Maru`
- はやぶさ(隼)号 → `Hayabusa Gō`
- プレジデント・ウィルソン号 → `Purejidento Wiruson Gō`

---

## 3. Abbreviated proper names (§6.9.1.3)

Write words consisting of or containing abbreviated proper names as single words.

### Examples

- 沙翁物語 → `Saō monogatari`
- 福翁百話 → `Fukuō hyakuwa`
- 産総研 → `Sansōken`

See also the geographic abbreviated-name rules in §6.10.8.

---

## 4. Well-established corporate names and areas of study (§6.9.2.1)

When a personal name is incorporated into a well-established corporate name, area of study, or similar established expression, follow the ordinary suffix rule and write the resulting compound together.

### Examples

- 陽明学 → `Yōmeigaku`
- 徂徠学 → `Soraigaku`
- 蘇峰会 → `Sohōkai`
- 小野組 → `Onogumi`

However, if the expression is judged to be a **one-off publication or event**, follow §6.9.1 and keep the affix separate.

- 芥川龍之介学 → `Akutagawa Ryūnosuke gaku`
- 漱石学 → `Sōseki gaku`

### Decision point

The distinction depends on whether the expression is an established entity/field or an ad hoc publication/event label.

---

## 5. Proper names whose reading changes to on'yomi (§6.9.2.2)

Write as a single-word compound when a proper name changes to an on'yomi reading as part of the compound.

### Examples

| Japanese | Romanization | Ordinary name reading noted by source |
|---|---|---|
| 義経記 | `Gikeiki` | Yoshitsune |
| 信長記 | `Shinchōki` | Nobunaga |
| 源氏 | `Genji` | Minamoto |
| 平家 | `Heike` | Taira |

But:

- 左氏 → `Sa-shi` because the reading of the Chinese family name `左` does not change as a result of `氏`.

### Decision point

The Skill must know both the normal proper-name reading and the compound reading; a changed reading is part of the rule condition.

---

## 6. Compound titles and terms of address (§6.9.2.3.1)

Write compound-word titles or terms of address separately from the personal name.

Also write separately phrases containing `の` that connect a name to a term of address.

### Examples

- 聖サビエル → `Sei Sabieru`
- 明治天皇 → `Meiji Tennō`
- 待賢門院 → `Taiken Mon'in`
- 竹沢先生 → `Takezawa Sensei`
- 北白川の宮様 → `Kitashirakawa no Miya-sama`
- 大岡越前守 → `Ōoka Echizen no Kami`

---

## 7. Single-character or kana title/address/plural suffix (§6.9.2.3.2)

When a title, term of address, or plural suffix following a personal name consists of a single character or kana unit, hyphenate it.

### Examples

- 芭蕉翁 → `Bashō-ō`
- 覚信尼 → `Kakushin-ni`
- 後鳥羽院 → `Gotoba-in`
- お菊さん → `Okiku-san`
- 中村君 → `Nakamura-kun`
- ノンちゃん雲に乗る → `Non-chan kumo ni noru`
- 竜馬たち → `Ryōma-tachi`

---

## 8. Contractions with personal names (§6.9.2.3.3)

Write as one word a contraction formed by adding a title or term of address to a personal name.

- さっちゃん → `Satchan`
- マッサン → `Massan`

This is an explicit exception to the hyphen rule above.

---

## 9. Families and clans (§6.9.2.3.4)

Hyphenate a suffix following a family or clan name.

- 徳川家 → `Tokugawa-ke`
- メディチ家 → `Medichi-ke`
- ハイド氏 → `Haido-shi`

---

## 10. Personal name + title + suffix (§6.9.2.3.5)

Hyphenate a suffix following a title and personal name.

### Examples

- 日蓮上人様 → `Nichiren Shōnin-sama`
- 悠仁親王様 → `Hisahito Shinnō-sama`
- 明治天皇家 → `Meiji Tennō-ke`
- 徳川将軍家 → `Tokugawa Shōgun-ke`

---

## 11. Geographic and other proper names (§6.9.3)

For geographic names and other proper names that are **not** personal names, corporate names, or titles of works, follow the general word-division rules and form compounds as appropriate.

### Examples

- 日本史 → `Nihonshi`
- フランス史 → `Furansushi`
- 日本茶 → `Nihoncha`
- 清朝 → `Shinchō`
- 明治期 → `Meijiki`

When a geographic name combines with another element to designate a jurisdiction, geographic feature, etc., use §6.10.

---

## 12. Prefixes with proper names (§6.9.4)

Write a prefix separately from a proper name.

### Examples

- 大日本帝国 → `Dai Nippon Teikoku` (suggested variant `Dai Nihon Teikoku`)
- 新東宝株式会社 → `Shin Tōhō Kabushiki Kaisha`
- 全日本観光連盟 → `Zen Nihon Kankō Renmei`
- 故黒澤明 → `ko Kurosawa Akira`
- 新ルネッサンス → `shin Runessansu`
- 新古今和歌集 → `Shin kokin wakashū`

Capitalization still follows the entity/title rules in §3.

---

## 13. Special single-character suffixes (§6.9.5)

Hyphenate single-character suffixes that can attach to proper names, including:

`的`, `型`, `式`, `流`, `産`, `製`, `派`, `系`, `本`, `版`, `戦`, `側`.

### Examples

- 日本的 → `Nihon-teki`
- 香港型 → `Honkon-gata`
- 田中千代式 → `Tanaka Chiyo-shiki`
- フランス側 → `Furansu-gawa`

---

## 14. Suggested Phase 1 decision procedure

```text
First classify the proper-name type:
personal / corporate / work title / geographic / other proper name.
↓
Apply the corresponding general §6.9 rule.
↓
Check for explicit exception:
- named vehicle + maru/gō
- abbreviated proper name
- established corporate/study compound
- special on'yomi reading
- title/term of address/plural suffix
- contraction
- family/clan suffix
- proper-name prefix
- special one-character suffix
↓
If the rule depends on whether usage is established, one-off, or entity-specific,
emit a judgment point with supporting evidence.
```

## 15. Automation notes

### Likely automatable

- Hyphenating listed single-character suffixes once proper-name status is known.
- Separating `maru`/`gō` for identified named vehicles.
- Hyphenating common address/plural suffixes after a personal name.
- Joining known contracted forms such as `Satchan` and `Massan`.

### May require contextual or reference-source judgment

- Classifying the entity type.
- Established field/corporate expression versus one-off publication/event.
- Detecting that a proper-name reading has changed to on'yomi in a compound.
- Distinguishing personal-name, clan/family, geographic, corporate, and work-title contexts.

### Open questions for Phase 1

- What entity-type metadata is normally available from cataloging input, and what must the Skill infer?
- Can proper-name type be passed explicitly to avoid unnecessary AI classification?
- How should “well-established” versus “one-off” usage be evidenced and recorded?
- Should the special proper-name suffix list become deterministic code immediately after Phase 1, or remain table-driven data?
